# Thought: video script (about 2:50)

Face on camera to open, then screen share: the deck first, then the live app.

- **Deck:** https://claude.ai/artifact/AArddBB5fQmX8318dpAnXJ (press Present, or use the arrow keys)
- **App:** https://thought-one.vercel.app (phone-size window, see "Set up" at the bottom)

Read it out loud once before recording. Talk like you're telling a friend, not reading. The lines in **bold** are the ones to land slowly.

## 1. Face on camera (0:00–0:15)

> Hi, I'm Summer.
>
> A lot of grandparents live across an ocean from their grandkids. When they want to send a gift, all they can do is wire money. And then they never see what it turned into.
>
> **So I built Thought: thoughtful gifts from grandparents abroad.**

*Switch to screen share.*

## 2. The story: slides (0:15–0:55)

**Slide 2, "It's 9 p.m. in Taipei"**

> Meet Grandma Chen. She lives in Taipei. Her granddaughter Mei is six, and lives in San Francisco.
>
> Last year, for Mei's birthday, Grandma sent a wire. A bank form, fees nobody explained, a week of waiting. Mei's mom said thank you for her.
>
> **Grandma never got to see what it bought.**

**Slide 3, "Distance is lonely"**

> And that's the part that hurts. Being far away is lonely. Research says what helps isn't calling more. It's feeling close.

**Slide 5, "Grandparents already give"** *(just a few seconds)*

> Grandparents already give. But sending money through a bank can cost almost fifteen percent.

**Slide 6, "Give a Saturday, not a transfer"**

> So instead of a transfer, what if Grandma could give a moment? A Saturday at the movies. Swim lessons. A night off for tired parents.

**Slide 7, "Three steps, three people"**

> Here's how it works. Grandma picks a gift. PayPal moves the money. And Mei says thank you. Let me show you.

*Switch to the app.*

## 3. Demo: the app (0:55–2:35)

**Start page → "Open as Grandma Chen"**

> This is Grandma's side. Everything is in Chinese, because that's her language.

**Tap a suggestion, like 博物館一年會員 (a museum year)** *(cut the wait in editing if it's slow)*

> She's not sure what to give, so she asks. Our AI knows Mei's age and her city, and suggests gifts that fit. It can suggest, but it can never pay. Only Grandma can.

**Open a gift → show the amounts → tap "PayPal 手續費 ›"**

> She picks an amount. Notice there are no fours. In Taiwan, four sounds like a bad word. Small thing, **but it says "we thought about you."**
>
> **And before she pays, she sees every fee. Nothing hidden.**

**Tap 用 PayPal 付款 → PayPal checkout → approve** *(cut the login)*

> She pays in her own money, Taiwan dollars, right on PayPal.

**Sent screen: the heart travels to Mei**

> And here's the receipt, with PayPal's actual numbers: the fee, the exchange rate, and exactly what Mei's family got.

**Back → "Open as Grandpa Kenji" → 土曜日は映画へ (Saturday at the movies)**

> Now, Grandpa Kenji lives in Tokyo. He gave to the same gift, in yen.
>
> **Two grandparents, two countries, one gift.** And each one only sees what they gave. **No scoreboard. It's from both of them.**

**Back → "Open as Mei" → Say thank you → record a few seconds → send**

> Now Mei. She sees one gift, from Grandma and Grandpa together. She records one little thank-you…
>
> …and the heart goes back to both of them.

**Back → "Open as Grandma Chen": Mei waving, 新的 (New) → press play**

> And on Grandma's phone, Mei's thank-you is waiting, translated into Chinese, calling her 阿嬤, the way Mei does.
>
> **That's the moment this whole thing is for.**

*Switch back to the deck.*

## Set up before recording (5 minutes)

1. **Reset the demo:** open the app's start page and click "Start the demo over" at the bottom.
2. **Phone-size window for the app:** in Chrome, open Developer Tools (⌥⌘I) → device toolbar (⌘⇧M) → iPhone 14 Pro Max. Or use your iPhone's screen recorder.
3. **Pay for a gift that isn't full yet**, like 讓爸媽放一晚假 (a night off for Mei's parents). The movie gift is already fully given.
4. **Have the Taiwan sandbox login ready.** Cut the typing out when you edit.
5. **Camera and mic:** good light for your face and for Mei's thank-you.
6. **No music,** unless it's royalty-free.
7. **Keep it under 3:00.** If you run long, shorten the slide part (skip slide 5) or the AI ask.
