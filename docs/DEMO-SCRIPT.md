# Demo video: Thought (target 2:45, limit 3:00)

Record the live site, https://thought-one.vercel.app, at phone size. That's the device it's built for.

## Before you record (10 min)

1. **Phone-size browser.** In Chrome: View → Developer → Developer Tools → device toolbar → iPhone 14 Pro Max (430 × 932). Or record on your iPhone with screen recording.
2. **Reset the demo:** start page → "Start the demo over".
3. **Pick the gift you'll pay for live:** 讓爸媽放一晚假 (sitter night) or 探索館一年會員 (Exploratorium). The movie gift is already fully given.
4. **Have the sandbox login ready** (Grandma Chen's Taiwan test account). Cut the typing out in editing.
5. **Camera on** for Mei's part, in good light.
6. **No music** unless it's royalty-free. No third-party logos beyond PayPal's own checkout.
7. Close other tabs and turn off notifications.

## The script

Spoken lines are in quotes. The screen notes say what to show.

| Time | Screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| **0:00–0:15** | Start page: the family and the logo | "It's 9 p.m. in Taipei. Grandma Chen wants to give her granddaughter Mei, who lives in San Francisco, something meaningful for her birthday. Until now, all she could send was a wire. Thought turns that wire into a thoughtful gift." |
| **0:15–0:40** | Open as Grandma Chen (Chinese). Type in the ask box: 下雨天適合送 Mei 什麼？ Show the answer. | "Everything is in her language. She can ask our AI concierge for ideas. It knows Mei's age, her city, and what her parents said she loves. It suggests gifts and amounts, but it can never pay. Only Grandma can." |
| **0:40–1:05** | Tap a gift. Show the three amounts. Tap "PayPal 手續費 ›" to open every fee. | "Amounts follow her customs: in Taiwan, no 4s, lucky 6s and 8s. Before she pays, she sees every fee, our zero, and what Mei's family receives." |
| **1:05–1:30** | 用 PayPal 付款 → PayPal sandbox checkout → approve (cut the login) → Sent screen; the heart travels to Mei. | "She pays in New Taiwan dollars on PayPal. Back in Thought, the receipt shows PayPal's actual numbers: the fee, PayPal's exchange rate, and the US dollars that arrived. Our estimate matches PayPal's fee exactly." |
| **1:30–1:50** | Back → Open as Grandpa Kenji (Japanese) → 土曜日は映画へ (fully given, everyone together). | "Grandpa Kenji in Tokyo gave yen to the same Saturday at the movies. Two countries, two currencies, one gift. Each grandparent sees only their own amount. No scoreboard. It's given together." |
| **1:50–2:20** | Open as Mei → Say thank you → record 5 seconds → Send → "Sent to 阿嬤 and おじいちゃん"; the heart travels to both. | "Mei sees one gift from her grandparents. She records one thank-you, and it goes to both of them." |
| **2:20–2:40** | Open as Grandma Chen → Mei waving, 新的 (New) → play the video; show the Chinese caption. | "On Grandma's phone, Mei's thank-you arrives, translated into Chinese, using the name Mei calls her. Grandpa gets it in Japanese." |
| **2:40–2:55** | Start page with the family | "Thought. Thoughtful gifts from grandparents abroad, built with PayPal and AI." |

**Tip:** if you're running long, cut the AI ask from 0:15–0:40 down to 15 seconds and say it over the answer as it appears.
