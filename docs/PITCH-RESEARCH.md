# Thought: pitch research

Facts used in the deck "Thought · Grandma got you Saturday". Each was checked on its source page (Nov 2026).

## Why it matters

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| 1 in 6 people worldwide are affected by loneliness, linked to about 871,000 deaths a year | [WHO Commission on Social Connection, 2025](https://www.who.int/groups/commission-on-social-connection) |
| Lacking social connection raises early-death risk about as much as smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day | [US Surgeon General advisory, 2023](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) |
| Emotional closeness between grandparents and grandchildren lowered depressive symptoms for both; more contact alone did not | [Moorman & Stokes, Boston College, 2016](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24906517/) |

|---|---|

## Why experiences and thank-yous

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Experiential gifts build more closeness than material gifts, even when the giver isn't there | [Chan & Mogilner, Journal of Consumer Research, 2017](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article-abstract/43/6/913/2632328) |
| People who write thank-you letters underestimate how much they mean to the person who gets them | [Kumar & Epley, Psychological Science, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29949445/) |
