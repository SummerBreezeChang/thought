# Thought: pitch research

Facts used in the deck "Thought · Grandma got you Saturday". Each was checked on its source page twice (Nov 10 and Nov 11, 2026). The second, independent check corrected two items: the closeness study and the red-envelope range.

## Why it matters

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| 1 in 6 people worldwide are affected by loneliness, linked to about 871,000 deaths a year | [WHO Commission on Social Connection, 2025](https://www.who.int/groups/commission-on-social-connection) |
| The mortality impact of social disconnection is similar to smoking up to 15 cigarettes a day | [US Surgeon General advisory, 2023](https://www.hhs.gov/sites/default/files/surgeon-general-social-connection-advisory.pdf) |
| For grandparents and adult grandchildren, feeling close lowered depressive symptoms; more frequent contact on its own was linked to slightly more | [Moorman & Stokes, The Gerontologist 56(3), 2016 (online 2014)](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24906517/) |

## Money

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| The average US grandparent spends $2,654 a year on all their grandchildren combined; 76% say texts, video calls and similar tech are a primary way they stay connected | [AARP, The Essential Role of Grandparents, 2026 (survey Nov–Dec 2025)](https://www.aarp.org/pri/topics/social-leisure/relationships/the-essential-role-of-grandparents/) |
| Sending $200 costs 6.36% on average worldwide; through banks it averages 14.99% | [World Bank Remittance Prices Worldwide, Q3 2025](https://remittanceprices.worldbank.org/sites/default/files/2026-04/RPW_main_report_and_annex_Q325.pdf) |
| Sending from Japan averages 9.98%, up from 7.11% | World Bank RPW, Q3 2025 (latest issue as of Nov 2026) |
| Taiwan red envelopes: even amounts, never a 4. (NT$3,600–6,600 is what adults give *to* grandparents, not what grandparents give.) | [CommonWealth Magazine, 2023](https://english.cw.com.tw/article/article.action?id=3370) |

## Why experiences and thank-yous

| Fact | Source |
|---|---|
| Experiential gifts build more closeness than material gifts, even when the giver isn't there | [Chan & Mogilner, Journal of Consumer Research, 2017](https://academic.oup.com/jcr/article-abstract/43/6/913/2632328) |
| People who write thank-you letters underestimate how positive the recipient will feel, and overestimate how awkward | [Kumar & Epley, Psychological Science, 2018](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29949445/) |
